Women’s Online Safety · 30 minutes

Protect yourself from deepfakes and image misuse.

AI-made or edited photos can be used to scare, shame or blackmail. This session teaches students how it happens, how to reduce the risk, and what to do if it happens to them or a friend.

1

Understand

What deepfakes and AI-generated images are, how to spot the signs, and why a photo online can be misused.

2

Protect

Privacy settings, thinking before posting or sending, who to accept, and keeping private photos private.

3

Respond

A clear plan for the moment someone threatens you, so students stay calm and are not alone with it.

If someone threatens you with a fake or private image

  1. Stay calm. It is not your fault. The person threatening you is the one doing wrong.
  2. Do not pay and do not send anything. Giving in usually brings more demands.
  3. Do not delete. Save screenshots, usernames, links and messages as proof.
  4. Block and report the account on the platform.
  5. Tell a trusted adult or teacher today. You should not handle this alone.
  6. Report it officially. Cyber crime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in. Childline 1098 for children.

Questions

Questions schools ask.

Will this take time away from studies?

A seminar is a single session, and a large part of it is about studying better with AI. Bootcamps run in school breaks.

Won’t students use AI to cheat?

That is why we teach it. Students see where AI makes mistakes and learn to use it for understanding, not copying.

How is the women’s safety session handled?

It is age-appropriate and calm. No explicit material is shown, the school counsellor is welcome, and students leave with clear steps and who to tell.

Which classes and boards?

Classes 8 to 12 with separate Junior and Senior versions. CBSE, ICSE and State boards are all fine because the content is not syllabus-based.

What does the school arrange?

A hall or classroom, a projector and sound system, a time slot and a coordinator teacher. We bring everything else.

Do students need laptops or phones?

No. Activities run on our devices and in teams. Nobody signs up for anything.
