What students take home

Six ideas, each learned through an activity they will remember.

How AI learns

AI finds patterns in examples. Students see why bad examples give bad answers.

Activity: teach a small model to tell two things apart, then break it.

Where AI already is

From video recommendations to maps to medicine. AI is much more than a chatbot.

Activity: a quick poll, then a hunt for AI inside everyday apps.

Asking better questions

A simple formula for prompts: who to be, what to do, for which level, in what format.

Activity: the Prompt Battle, live on stage.

Studying with AI

Understand a topic, practise with quizzes, then check the answers. AI helps learning when it does not replace thinking.

Activity: turn a chapter into a quiz, then catch AI making a mistake.

Staying safe

Spotting fake images and fake news, and knowing what never to share with an app.

Activity: a team game of real versus AI-made.

The road ahead

How work is changing, and which skills matter from Class 8 to 12.

Activity: a guest talk and open Q&A with an industry expert.

Questions schools ask

Will this take time away from studies?

It is a single session, and a large part of it is about studying better with AI: understanding topics, practising, and checking answers.

Won’t students just use AI to cheat?

That is exactly why we teach it. Students see where AI makes mistakes and learn to use it for understanding rather than copying.

Which classes is it for?

Classes 8 to 12. The flow is the same, with separate examples for Juniors (8–10) and Seniors (11–12). CBSE, ICSE and State boards are all fine because the content is not syllabus-based.

How long is the bootcamp?

About 2.5 hours including a short break. We can adjust the plan to fit your school’s timetable.

How many students can attend?

We can run it for a whole school, in batches if needed, for example Juniors and Seniors in separate sessions.

What does the school need to arrange?

A hall, a projector, a sound system and a time slot, ideally during school hours. Our team brings the rest.

Do students need laptops or phones?

No. Activities run on our devices and in teams, so students do not need to bring anything or create accounts.

What about fees?

We discuss this with you on the demo call, so the arrangement suits your school.
